THE LAST-OUT MOMENT · THE ANCHOR
01 / THE IDEA

Make the right action
the easy action.

Students are often in a hurry, distracted, or unsure who is responsible. P15 places a clear cue at the exact point where leaving becomes a decision.

MVP HYPOTHESIS

If students encounter a high-contrast reminder at the exit, at least 60% of last-exiting students will check and switch off active lights and fans within 10 seconds without verbal prompting.

02 / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Four artefacts.
One clear routine.

Each physical component solves a specific point of friction discovered during field testing in Academic Block 34.

01

The Anchor / Handle Tag

A neon-yellow laminated acrylic tag sits directly over the primary interior lever handle, interrupting the exit motion with a quick visual reminder.

8.5 × 22 cm · 3.5 cm handle cutout · Rubber grommet reinforcement
02

The Safeguard / Jamb Sticker

An eye-level laminated vinyl strip on the interior door jamb keeps the prompt visible when the door is propped open.

Laminated vinyl · Door-frame edge · Backup visual cue
03

The Guide / Floor Decals

Removable arrows and footprint outlines lead students from the central aisle toward the exit and switchboard zone.

Low-tack vinyl · Yellow + matte gray · Starts 2 metres from exit
04

The Reducer / Switchboard Strip

A fixture-mapping schematic makes the switchboard easier to understand, reducing hesitation and trial-and-error toggling.

Mounted at 1.5 m · Blue fan icons · Yellow bulb icons · 30% larger font
03 / USER SERVICE JOURNEY

From pack-up
to lights out.

A five-step sequence designed to fit naturally into the transition between classes.

Pack-up

Lecture ends. Students gather belongings and prepare to leave.

Floor Cue

Footprints and arrows guide attention toward the exit and switchboard.

Door Nudge

The last-out student sees the bright handle tag and jamb prompt.

Switch Check

The schematic strip maps fixtures to switches. Turn off active lights and fans.

Leave

Exit with minimal friction. If another class is next, leave the AC on.

04 / WEEK 11 → WEEK 12

Test. Notice.
Fix the friction.

Field testing in Academic Block 34, Classrooms 34-201 and 34-204, informed four physical improvements.

Issue Problem Severity Physical Fix
ISS-01 Tag hidden when door was propped open High Added an auxiliary sticker directly to the interior door jamb.
ISS-02 Cardstock tore around handle cutout High Upgraded to laminated acrylic with a heavy-duty rubber grommet.
ISS-03 Uncertainty about back-to-back classes Medium Added explicit policy: Cut lights/fans, leave AC on.
ISS-04 Switchboard icons hard to read Medium Increased font scale by 30% and added high-contrast outlines.
